    why this mattersNames are resolved by whoever the tunnel hands them to. If this sits in your real country while the exit does not, queries are escaping the chain - a leak neither the exit address nor the shell can see. The resolver is anycast, so its apparent location tracks where the query entered the network rather than the operator's head office.
